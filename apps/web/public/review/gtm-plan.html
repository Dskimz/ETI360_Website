Section 1

The position.

ETI360 is an independent trip-governance service for international schools and educational travel providers.

Positioning statement ETI360 provides independent trip-governance services that structure the evidence behind every trip decision, because the same documented framework is applied to the organization once a year, to each trip before departure, and to the trip while it runs, producing complete documents the school can read and hold, unlike provider-written paperwork, approval software that records a decision without producing its evidence, and enterprise assistance services priced for corporate travel.

The short descriptor opens every surface; the full statement is the standard it compresses. Together they are the entire message, used identically on the homepage, the LinkedIn profiles, the emails, and in the sales conversation. No slogan exists anywhere in the campaign.

Section 2

The money.

The first trip is not the win. A school with fifty trips is.

~$750per reviewed trip, the working ballpark
$37,500a year from one fifty-trip school
$750annual Organizational Baseline; offered cheaply as the door-opener for non-clients
$1,000Duty Manager Simulation, once trips are in the system

Revenue is the program, not the project: land inexpensively, review one trip well, then take up the calendar. The account renews itself through the annual baseline and next year’s trips, and the re-run engine is what makes trip thirty as profitable as trip three. Dashboard access is annual and included in the cost of the trips. When a provider is the client, the provider pays for its own baseline. Prices live in proposals and conversations, never on a public page. The itemized menu is PRICING-MENU.html in the checkout root, deliberately not web-served, internal, for Seb’s fine pass.

The ladder

  1. Door-opener: the Organizational Baseline at an introductory price, for schools and providers not yet clients.
  2. First trip: one full trip review at the working price. The demonstration is paid.
  3. The program: the year’s calendar at per-trip pricing, Dashboard included while trips run.
  4. The Simulation: after the trips are in the system, because it runs on the school’s real trip data.
  5. Renewal: the baseline refresh and the next calendar.

Section 3

The campaign.

The campaign is the 3-Tier framework itself, drawn as a timeline with the feedback loop: the annual foundation, each individual trip producing its Decision File, the school’s own team while trips are underway, and the findings carried back into next year. The centerpiece graphic is built with real Harborview documents at framework-timeline.html, and it becomes the homepage band, the spine of /framework, the visual structure of arm C, and the standing LinkedIn carousel.

Content is the framework walked tier by tier through finished artifacts: the eight-post story first, with its recorded corrections, then one artifact-led post per week. Founder posts carry reach in first person; the company page holds the record; Facebook gets the pinned identity post and the four cuts, then nothing. Every asset ends at the same door: a trip-readiness briefing.

Voice, unchanged and binding: celebrate the trip and the craft, plain working prose, no fear framing, no urgency, no slogans, and the boundary stated proudly rather than buried.

Section 4

The market.

Who. International schools with substantial trip programs; the account thesis makes program size the first qualifier. The directory holds 510 schools, 165 with documented trip programs; within each wave, schools with the strongest program evidence go first.

Order. Latin America first, then the world, 50 schools a week, as ruled. School-first for 90 days; providers follow once the first school is signed, and are then sold the same services, paying for their own baselines.

Entry points. Leadership owns the year and receives the commercial ask; with per-document pricing a coordinator can also start a single trip without a budget conversation. Both routes land in the same briefing.

Proof. The Harborview sample file, complete and disclosed as fictitious, ungated. No case studies until a real client grants one.
