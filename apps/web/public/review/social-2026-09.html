Post 2 · The 3-Tier Risk FrameworkTue Sep 8
ETI360
Educational Travel Insights · Following
A school trip is a sequence of decisions. The framework follows them. Tier 1, the Organizational Baseline, is where the school and its providers stand before the year's trips begin: one documented review of readiness, refreshed annually. Tier 2, the Trip Risk Review, is what leadership sees before a trip is approved: a consistent set of documents for every trip, from the trip overview to the information parents receive. Tier 3, Dynamic Risk Operations, is how the school stays connected while a group travels — and the record the trip carries home into next year's planning. #RiskGovernance #EducationalTravel

Post 4 · Tier 2 · The trip fileTue Sep 15
ETI360

What leadership sees before a trip is approved. Every trip arrives at sign-off as the same consistent file: the trip overview, the itinerary day by day with nothing unaccounted for, and the risk information organized the way the trip actually runs — one report per activity group, with hazards, controls, and who holds each control in plain language.
