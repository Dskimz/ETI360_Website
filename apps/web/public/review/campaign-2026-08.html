01The campaign at a glance

Sent as personal Gmail messages: drafts are prepared per school, Dan reviews each one and sends. No mail platform, no merge tags, a plain reply-to-opt-out line.
Channel 2 · SocialOne story, both platforms: eight posts over four weeks, two per week, telling the work in order (introduction, the framework, then each tier through its documents to the record afterward). The same post runs on LinkedIn and on Facebook in a shorter cut; formats differ, the story does not. Organic only; no paid until email shows traction.
MeasurementEvery link is tagged: utm_source = email / linkedin / facebook · utm_campaign = intro · utm_content = note / marquee / catalogue / post-1 … post-8. Email drafts additionally carry utm_term = school, so arm-vs-arm and school-level clicks are comparable in analytics. Site analytics today: Vercel Web Analytics, cookieless, live.
CadenceWeek 1: email wave 1 + Posts 1 and 2. Week 2: wave 2 + Posts 3 and 4. Week 3: wave 3 + Posts 5 and 6. Week 4: Posts 7 and 8, then a hold-or-continue review on the numbers.

02Tone and voice

The rules every piece below follows. Full document: docs/ETI360_TONE_AND_VOICE.md.

  • Register: Economist-influenced: evidence-based, restrained, precise. The feel of International SOS or Crisis24, never EdTech, generic SaaS, government-compliance gray, or adventure-travel lifestyle. American English everywhere.
  • Moment-first, benefit-led: every piece opens on the reader's moment (the sign-off meeting, the days the group is away, the phone held overnight, the page a parent reads): never on what a school lacks, never fear or liability framing, never document counts.
  • Output-first doctrine: show what the school receives: the page, the screen, the measured figure: before explaining how the system works. Real artifacts over descriptions; actual product renders over recreations.
  • Governance boundary: ETI360 structures, identifies, surfaces, and organizes; it never advises, certifies, approves, or guarantees. The decision stays with the school. Banned verbs in claims: approves, certifies, ensures safety, manages risk on your behalf.
  • Specificity over adjectives: exact numbers when available (11.1 km, 882 m net ascent, 43.5% steepest window), sourced where possible; never superlatives (best, leading, world-class), never urgency (act now, don't miss), never unverifiable comparative claims.
  • Banned register: emojis, exclamation marks, SaaS vocabulary (seamless, powerful, cutting-edge), EdTech vocabulary (empowers, transforms, unlocks potential), borrowed jargon (operational tempo, consume, cross-mapping): plain English replacements are cataloged in the tone doc.
  • Naming canon: Tier 1 Organizational Baseline / Tier 2 Trip Risk Review / Tier 3 Dynamic Risk Operations ('Assurance' retired); 'Duty Manager Dashboard' (never 'Live Ops Centre'); 'trips' never 'fleet'; 'incident type' never 'event family'; Baseline shows 'At standard / Progressing' states, never percentages.
  • Harborview International School is the fictitious reference school: disclosed as fictitious every time an HIS artifact is shown ('fictitious by design, so no real school's documents are ever shown'). The 'Start with one trip' slogan is retired as a tagline (Dan, 2026-08-25): the one-trip offer is phrased plainly, and the gold brand line is 'Risk intelligence for school trips'.

Paid or organic

Linkedin

  • [General platform knowledge] A new company page starts with near-zero organic distribution: LinkedIn shows company-page posts to a small fraction of followers, and a page starting under a few hundred followers generates negligible impressions without amplification.
  • [General platform knowledge] Personal profiles reach materially further than company pages; a founder's post typically earns several times the impressions of the identical post on a young company page, so the page's early role is credibility and an archive, not reach.
  • [General platform knowledge] LinkedIn ad minimums are about US$10/day per campaign, and CPCs for narrow senior B2B audiences commonly run US$8-15 or more, so a readable test needs roughly US$500-1,000 over 2-4 weeks.
  • [General platform knowledge] Native targeting fits school leadership: job titles (Head of School, Principal, Director of Operations, Business Manager/Bursar, Trip or Activities Coordinator), industry (Primary/Secondary Education), and uploaded company lists of international schools: but these audiences are small, which raises CPMs and slows learning.
  • [General platform knowledge] Retargeting site visitors and matched-list audiences require the Insight Tag on eti360.com and a minimum audience of about 300 matched members, which a pre-launch site will take time to accumulate.
  • [Repo fact] The three email arms in dev/schools-email/ are finished but marked NOT YET SENT: awaiting green light: so there is no demand signal yet for paid spend to amplify.
  • [Repo fact] The destination and creatives already exist and are live (/for-schools plus the /email/ and /marketing/library/ renders), so organic posting costs nothing beyond scheduling and reuses tone-gated material.
Recommendation: Run weeks 1-3 organic only: posts carried by the personal profile with the company page as the archive: and revisit paid only after the email arms have been sent and /for-schools shows engaged UTM-tagged visits; if that signal appears, a first test of about US$750 over 3-4 weeks against a titles-plus-geography audience and site retargeting is the smallest spend likely to produce readable data.

Facebook

  • [General platform knowledge] Organic reach for business pages sits in the low single digits of followers for most pages, and a brand-new page effectively starts at zero; posts linking off-platform are further deprioritized.
  • [General platform knowledge] Facebook cannot target job titles with LinkedIn's reliability; school-leadership audiences must be approximated through interests, lookalikes, and geography: a fit for parent-facing consumer messaging, not Head-of-School outreach.
  • [General platform knowledge] Meta ad costs are much lower (CPCs commonly under US$2), so a readable test costs US$200-400 over a few weeks: but cheap clicks from the wrong audience are not progress for a governance consultancy.
  • [General platform knowledge] The realistic organic value of a school-facing Facebook page is verification: leaders and school communities who hear of ETI360 look it up, and an active page with real artifact imagery supports credibility at that moment.
  • [General platform knowledge] School communities do follow school and education pages on Facebook, so well-made posts can travel through shares even when page reach is minimal.
  • [Repo fact] The campaign canon (moment-first, benefit-led, no fear framing, no engagement bait) rules out the participation-bait formats Facebook's ranking rewards, capping expected organic reach by design.
  • [Repo fact] Same sequencing fact as LinkedIn: the email arms are unsent, so there is no traction data to justify any Facebook spend yet.
Recommendation: Treat Facebook as a credibility surface, not an acquisition channel: publish the four posts on schedule, keep the page details current, and spend nothing; consider only a small retargeting-only test (about US$300 cap) after email and LinkedIn identify a converting message, and skip Facebook paid entirely if LinkedIn retargeting already covers the audience.

Decision recorded: organic only on both platforms; no paid spend for this campaign.

06How the pieces run

The plain-language version of where everything lives, for orientation rather than approval.

The websitewww.eti360.com is a Next.js site hosted on Vercel. When code is pushed to its GitHub repository, Vercel rebuilds and publishes the site in about two minutes. Everything a prospect sees from the emails and posts lives here.
The working appThe review app (the school portal, the Duty Manager Dashboard, the document packs) is a separate application hosted on Render at eti360-review.onrender.com. It deploys from its own repository. This is where "request access" invitations land.
The domainThe eti360.com domain and its DNS records are managed at GoDaddy. The www address points at Vercel. Planned next step: point app.eti360.com at the Render app, so client-facing links carry our own domain instead of onrender.com. One DNS record plus one setting in Render; about ten minutes.
AnalyticsVercel Web Analytics is live: cookieless, it reports page views, referrers, visitor country, and device, which is where the campaign UTM tags land. Google Analytics is wired in the code but switched off. Because the site sets no cookies today, no cookie consent banner is required; switching Google Analytics on would change that, and the privacy page is already worded for both states.
EmailCampaign emails go out as personal Gmail messages, drafted per school and reviewed by Dan before sending. The website contact form delivers through Resend to danskimin@eti360.com; a live delivery test is on the pre-send checklist.
WhatsAppThe technical integration (Meta Cloud API: inbound webhook, outbound messages, emergency detection) is built and in the app. What remains is account-side: which phone number carries it, noting that a number cannot run the WhatsApp Business app and the API at the same time, plus confirming the four Meta keys on the Render server. Until then the campaign makes no WhatsApp claims.
