Section 2

Position and voice.

The sentence, used everywhereETI360 is the independent trip-governance service for international schools and the providers.
The full positionETI360 provides independent trip-governance services that structure the evidence behind every trip decision, because the same documented framework is applied to the organization once a year, to each trip before departure, and to the trip while it runs, producing complete documents the school can read and hold, unlike provider-written paperwork, approval software that records a decision without producing its evidence, and enterprise assistance services priced for corporate travel.

The framework says the rest: ETI360 helps schools establish organizational readiness, review each trip on its own merits, and equip their own teams for dynamic operations. The school retains the decision and the duty of care.

The rules every piece follows

  • Moment first, benefit led. Open on the reader's moment, never on what a school lacks. No fear framing, no liability framing, no urgency, no document counts.
  • Output first. Show what the school receives before explaining how the system works. Real pages, never mockups.
  • The boundary, stated plainly. ETI360 structures, identifies, surfaces and organizes. It never advises, certifies, approves or guarantees.
  • Specific over adjectival. Exact figures where they exist. No superlatives, no unverifiable comparisons.
  • Naming. Tier 1 Organizational Baseline, Tier 2 Trip Risk Review, Tier 3 Dynamic Risk Operations. The Duty Manager Dashboard. Trips, never fleet. No external standard named on a customer surface. Baseline areas show at standard or progressing, never a score.
  • Harborview International School is the reference school, disclosed as fictitious every time its work is shown.
  • American English. No em-dashes, no emojis, no exclamation marks.

One claim to confirm while you read: the arms state a combined thirty-plus years and more than ten thousand students. The years are right for the two of us. The student figure should have something behind it before it goes to fifty schools.

Section 3

What we sell, and for how much.

The first trip is not the win. A school with fifty trips is.

Revenue is the program, not the project. Land inexpensively, review one trip properly, then take up the calendar. The account renews through the annual baseline and next year's trips.

The ladder

  1. Door opener. The Organizational Baseline at an introductory price, for schools we are not yet working with.
  2. First trip. One trip review at the working price. The demonstration is paid for.
  3. The program. The year's calendar at per-trip pricing, with Dashboard access included while trips run.
  4. The Simulation. Once the school's trips are in the system, because it runs on their real trip data.
  5. Renewal. The baseline refresh and the next calendar.

What the Duty Manager Simulation actually is

It runs after a school's trips are in the system: itineraries built, procedures researched, risk assessments added, family documents produced. The session then uses their real trip data. Their duty manager works a timeline of those trips on the Duty Manager Dashboard, handling events as they arrive, to test their own procedures and their team's ability to run them. It is the second sale to an existing client, never the first sale to a stranger, which is why it does not appear in these emails.

No price appears on any public page or in any email. Prices live in proposals and conversations.

How each one now closes

This is the change. No arm offers to produce anything without charge.

Arm BIf you have a trip like this coming up, reply and we can talk through what a file for that trip would contain: the route pages, the assessment, the version families read.

Two further corrections made this week

  • Arm B said the route page recommends a walking pace. ETI360 records; it does not recommend. It now reads "the page records a planning pace of 1.5 to 2.0 kilometers per hour."
  • Arm C carried "Or start with one trip," which was retired as a slogan on 25 August. Gone.

Link tagging

All fourteen links across the three arms now carry one scheme, so replies and visits can be traced to the arm and the school that produced them.

The schemeutm_source=school-outreach · utm_medium=email · utm_campaign=latam-w1
utm_content=arm-a | arm-b | arm-c · utm_term=SCHOOL-SLUG

utm_term is replaced with the school's slug when each draft is written. utm_medium was absent from every link before this pass, which is the parameter analytics uses to group channels; without it the whole campaign would have arrived as unattributed traffic.

Section 7

How the pieces run.

The website

www.eti360.com is a Next.js site on Vercel. A push to the main branch rebuilds and publishes in about two minutes. Everything the emails and posts point to lives here.

Email

Campaign email goes out as personal Gmail messages, drafted per school and reviewed before sending. The website contact form delivers through Resend to Dan, and now also sends the sender an automatic acknowledgment signed by Dan.

Analytics

Vercel Web Analytics is live and cookieless, so the site sets no cookies and needs no consent banner. Campaign parameters land here. Google Analytics is wired but switched off; turning it on would change the cookie position.

The working app

The school portal, the Duty Manager Dashboard and the document packs run as a separate application on Render. Planned next step: move it to app.eti360.com so client links carry our own domain.

What is new on the site this week

  • The framework runs as a scrolling sequence on the homepage, one tier at a time.
  • A page for the Duty Manager Simulation.
  • One action site wide: Arrange a briefing.
  • Every external standard reference removed from customer pages.
  • The eight-page current baseline replaces the old one-page version.
