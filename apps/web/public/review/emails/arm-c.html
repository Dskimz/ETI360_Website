We have spent a combined thirty-plus years in educational travel risk and have sent more than ten thousand students on trips around the world. That experience informs our 3-Tier Risk Management Framework: a structured approach to preparation, trip review, and live operations. The work serves two moments: the day a trip is put in front of leadership, and the days the group is away.

Below are three pieces of that work. The full library, from the annual baseline to the post-trip record, is on our site: the documents open as complete PDFs, and the live operations screens are shown as working views.
1

Organizational Baseline

Where the school and its providers stand before the year's trips begin: one documented review of readiness, refreshed annually.
2

Trip Risk Review

What leadership sees before a trip is approved: a consistent set of documents for review and decision, every trip.
3

Dynamic Risk Operations

How the school stays connected while a group travels: the duty manager's working view, the agreed escalation path, and the post-trip record.
