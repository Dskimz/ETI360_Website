How the work runs

ETI360 is the independent trip-governance service for international schools and the providers.

The service runs on one framework across the school year. The organization is established once a year, each trip is reviewed on its own merits before departure, and the school's own teams are equipped for the days a group is away. When the group comes home, the record opens the next year.

Tier One · Once a year

Organizational Baseline

Establish organizational readiness.

Tier Two · Every trip

Trip Risk Review

Tier Three · During and after

Dynamic Risk Operations

Equip the school's own teams for dynamic operations.

What the loop does for us

It answers the objection that started this. A file is delivered and finished; this returns to the start, which is only something a service can do. The feedback loop is the annual relationship drawn rather than claimed, so nothing in the copy has to argue for it.

Your sentence is doing the labeling

Each tier carries one clause of it: establish, review, equip. Read the three verb lines across and you have your sentence back. That ties the words you approved to the picture without a slogan anywhere on the page.

Proportions are honest

Tier 2 is the widest band because that is where most of the work happens, once per trip. Tier 1 is one review a year applied to two kinds of organization. Tier 3 is what the school’s own people hold while a group is away. The widths tell the truth about the service before anyone reads a word.
