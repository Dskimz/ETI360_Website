Independent trip governance

ETI360 is the independent trip-governance service for international schools and the providers.

ETI360 helps schools establish organizational readiness, review each trip on its own merits, and equip their own teams for dynamic operations.

Tier 1 · Annual

Organizational Baseline

A standing view of governance, responsibilities, procedures, and capabilities that every trip review can reference.

Standing evidence Organizational Baseline What the organization already holds and where further work may be needed. The same review is applied to each provider the school travels with.

Tier 2 · Per trip

Trip Risk Review

Each trip is reviewed on its own merits, producing a Decision File for leadership review and operational handover.

The Decision File One trip, prepared for each reader and decision. Every document below is produced from the trip’s single record, and reissued from it when something changes.

The school’s own team works from a current operating picture, structured check-ins, and practical reference tools.
