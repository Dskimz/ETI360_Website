The service, written for /for-providers

Draft copy · the college-tour proposal serviceFor college-tour providers, the program-on-file model prepares the whole proposal cycle. When a school requests a tour, the itinerary and its reports are re-dated for the school's dates. Coordination emails for the hotels, restaurants, and transport around the tour are drafted from the same record. And the requests to each college for tour times are prepared, ready for you to review and send. Once the program is on file, every proposal after that is an adjustment, not a rebuild.

This matches the pattern you set on 4 August for the provider re-run service: offered now, cost silent in copy, no turnaround claim, and you verify bookings before anything is confirmed.

One honesty boundary

Selling this as a live offering is clean: the moment it is on the page, it is offered; the re-dating machinery exists, and the two email-drafting components are a bounded build completed before the first order. What the copy should not do is claim TSCT already receives it, because they do not yet, and a one-line check from them would undo the credibility the rest of the site is built on. So the copy above sells the service in present tense without naming a client; the week-12 TSCT conversation asks two things: first order, and written permission to name them. Service is never conditioned on the permission.

The five conversations, week 12

  • Existing relationships only, TSCT first. Not a campaign, not a list build.
  • Script: the college-tour service above, plus the both-sides argument already on the site: the same ten-area review a school holds, applied to the provider, in the form school committees already read.
