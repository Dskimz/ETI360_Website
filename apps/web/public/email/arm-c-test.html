Dear Dan,

We have spent a combined thirty-plus years in educational travel risk and have sent more than ten thousand students on trips around the world. That experience informs our 3-Tier Risk Management Framework: a structured approach to preparation, trip review, and live operations.

What follows is the document set prepared for a school's own trips, with defined information for school leaders, trip leaders, parents, and risk managers. If you would like to see how this would look for your school, contact us, or simply reply to this email.
1

Tier One · Annual

Organizational Baseline

A documented review of organizational readiness, applied to schools and to trip providers: policies, roles, evidence, and standing arrangements across ten areas, mapped against ISO 31031.

T1 · Schools and trip providers

Organizational Baseline Evaluation

The same ten-area review is applied to your school and to each trip provider who serves it: a baseline of organizational readiness on both sides of every trip. Policies, roles, standing arrangements, and supporting evidence are recorded in a common structure and refreshed annually.
