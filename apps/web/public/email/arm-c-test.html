An introduction

What a reviewed trip looks like

You run the trips. This is the documentation that sits underneath them.

Dear Dan,

I am Dan Skimin, co-founder of ETI360. I have spent fifteen years working with schools and their trips, and that time has taught me how much varies from one school to the next. You will know your students, your destinations and your staff far better than I could.

Where we may be able to help is the paperwork underneath: the risk assessment your board reads, the itinerary checked hour by hour, and the guide the teacher carries on the day. We work to a three tier risk framework, and each tier produces documents built for your school and your trip. Here is the full set.

1

Tier One · Annual

Organizational Baseline

The policies, approvals and provider checks a school already holds, set out in one place and mapped against ISO 31031.

 
     

T1

Organizational Baseline Evaluation

Your organization's standing position across ten capability areas, from approval gates and staffing ratios to provider due diligence and incident response. Each area is scored, the evidence is cited, and findings are ordered by consequence so you can decide what, if anything, is worth changing.

Learn more ›
     
           
   
2

Tier Two · Every trip

Trip Risk Review

One journey, documented end to end. The evidence behind an approval decision, from the risk assessment to the page parents read.

   
   

T2

Trip Risk Assessment & RAMS

The approval document. Hazards, the controls that address them, who holds each control, and residual risk set out in plain language.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Route Intelligence

For any walking, cycling or expedition leg. Terrain, gradient, exposure, surface, and the escape points available along the way, measured rather than described.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Parent Itinerary

Written for families, in the school's own voice. The trip on one page: what the students will be doing, where they stay, and how care is arranged.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Itinerary Report

A minute by minute account of where each group is across the whole trip, transit included, checked for full time coverage.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Weather & Conditions

Climate normals, seasonal hazards and daylight windows for your destination in your travel month, translated into kit, timings and contingency triggers.

Learn more ›
       
           
 
3

Tier Three · While it runs

Dynamic Risk Operations

What happens once the group is moving. The guide in the trip leader's hand, and the dashboard on the duty manager's desk at school.

     
 

T3

Teacher Operational Guide

Written for the teacher leading the group. One page per day: the run sheet, the learning intent for each place, a read and do emergency page, and the nearest hospital with local guidance on reaching it.
