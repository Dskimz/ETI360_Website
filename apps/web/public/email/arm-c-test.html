Dear Dan,

We have spent a combined thirty-plus years in educational travel risk and have sent more than ten thousand students on trips around the world. That experience became the 3-Tier Risk Management Framework and the systems around it: a service that helps schools strengthen how trips are prepared, monitored, and improved.

What follows are the documents and live systems the Framework produces for a school's own trips, built for students, teachers, parents, and risk managers. If you would like to see how this would look for your school, contact us, or simply reply to this email.

Tier One · Annual

Organizational Baseline

The standing capability of your school and of the partners who run your trips, each scored across ten areas and mapped against ISO 31031.

  
     

T1 · Your school

Organizational Baseline Evaluation

Your school's standing position across ten capability areas, scored on the evidence reviewed and tracked on the platform. Findings are ordered by consequence so you can decide what, if anything, is worth changing.

T1 · Your partners

Partner Baseline Evaluation

The same ten-area discipline, applied to the partners who run your trips. Each provider's standing capability is scored and evidenced, so the organizations your students travel with are as visible as your own.

Learn more ›
      
             
   
2

Tier Two · Every trip

Trip Risk Review

One journey, documented end to end. The evidence behind an approval decision, from the risk assessment to the page parents read.

    
   

T2

Trip Risk Assessment & RAMS

The approval document. Hazards, the controls that address them, who holds each control, and residual risk set out in plain language.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Route Intelligence

Every walking and cycling leg, measured rather than described: gradient, pacing, sun, terrain cover, and the escape points along the way. Shown complete: the Big Itoshima ride.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Trip Discovery Map

The whole trip on one sheet: every stop in order, the route between them, and the shape of each day. Shown complete: Cherry Blossom Tokyo.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Parent Itinerary

Written for families, in the school's own voice. What the students will be doing, where they stay, and how care is arranged.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Itinerary Report

A minute by minute account of where each group is across the whole trip, transit included, checked for full time coverage.

Learn more ›
 

T2

Weather & Conditions

Climate normals, seasonal hazards and daylight windows for your destination in your travel month, translated into kit, timings and contingency triggers.

Learn more ›
        
             
 
3

Tier Three · While it runs

Dynamic Risk Operations

What happens once the group is moving: the guide in the trip leader's hand, the dashboard on the duty manager's desk, the rehearsal before departure, and the loop that carries lessons home.

      
 

T3

Teacher Operational Guide

Written for the teacher leading the group. One page per day: the run sheet, the learning intent for each place, a read and do emergency page, and the nearest hospital with local guidance on reaching it.

Learn more ›
 

T3 · Live surface

Duty Manager Dashboard

The live surface your team operates while groups travel: check-ins, weather flags, incident logging, and the escalation path your school agreed before departure.

Learn more ›
 

T3 · Rehearsal

Duty Manager Simulation

A facilitated ninety-minute rehearsal inside the dashboard. Your duty manager works a realistic scenario built from one of your own trips, before ever facing a real one.

Learn more ›
 

T3 · The loop home

Post-Trip Feedback Loop

When the group lands, the Post-Trip Feedback Loop gathers what your staff observed while it is fresh and carries it back into next year's baseline.
